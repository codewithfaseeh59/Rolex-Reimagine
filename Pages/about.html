Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

The Visionary

Lucien Marchand

In 1926, a young Lucien Marchand founded the atelier that would become AURELIAN. His vision was simple yet ambitious — to place a reliable and precise watch on every wrist. What followed was a century of innovation that shaped how we think about watchmaking.

Marchand's relentless pursuit of perfection led to a series of landmark inventions — the sealed Halcyon case, the self-winding Aurum rotor, and the Voyager dual-time complication.

Our Journey

100 Years of History

1926

The Beginning

Lucien Marchand and Adèle Roux found the atelier in Neuchâtel, with a vision to create reliable wristwatches.

1934

First Precision Certificate

An AURELIAN calibre earns its first in-house precision certificate, setting the standard for every watch that follows.

1941

Halcyon Case

AURELIAN patents the Halcyon — a sealed, dustproof wristwatch case.

1952

Aurum Rotor

AURELIAN introduces the self-winding Aurum rotor — a revolution in the atelier's watchmaking.

1964

Abyss

The Abyss is introduced — our first diver's watch, waterproof to 100 metres.

1971

Circuit

The Circuit chronograph is launched — destined to become the atelier's most celebrated sports watch.

1998

New Manufacture

A purpose-built manufacture opens, bringing case, dial and movement making under one roof.

2026

One Hundred Years

AURELIAN marks a century of crafting timepieces that define elegance, performance and enduring value.
