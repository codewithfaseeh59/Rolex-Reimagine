The Art of Making

The AURELIAN Process

Our Philosophy

Where Science Meets Art

Every AURELIAN watch is a testament to the art of fine watchmaking. From the selection of raw materials to the final quality control, each step of the manufacturing process reflects our uncompromising pursuit of excellence.

01

Movement

In-House Movements

Every AURELIAN movement is designed, developed, and manufactured entirely in-house. This complete integration of the production process ensures uncompromising quality at every stage of the watchmaking process.

02

Materials

Aurasteel Mastery

AURELIAN uses its own exclusive alloy — Aurasteel — a high-chromium steel developed in our atelier. Highly resistant to corrosion, it acquires a remarkable sheen when polished.

03

Precision

Precision Certified

Every AURELIAN watch is tested over fifteen days and certified accurate to -3/+3 seconds per day — a standard that goes far beyond common industry requirements.

04

Innovation

Aurum Rotor

Our self-winding mechanism with the Aurum rotor, introduced in 1952, transformed our watchmaking. It winds the mainspring continuously through the natural motion of the wearer's wrist.
